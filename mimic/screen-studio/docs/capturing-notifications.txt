Troubleshooting
Capturing notifications
Sometimes you may need to capture notifications in a recording. By default, the notifications from the Notification Center are prevented from appearing in your recordings. However, you can easily change that setting whenever you need.
How to turn notifications on
To enable capturing notifications, go to System Settings and choose "Notifications" from the menu bar on the left. Next, click on “Allow notifications when mirroring or sharing display”.
