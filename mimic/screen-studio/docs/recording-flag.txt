Marking Recording Flag
To mark a recording flag during your session, use the default keyboard shortcut  ⌘  ⌥  ⌃ + F  . This will allow you to flag important moments in your recording. You will see these moments marked with a flag on your timeline, which will make it easier to identify key sections for editing.
Changing Shortcut For Recording Flag
If you want to change the shortcut for creating a recording flag, follow these steps:
Go to the top menu bar and click on   Screen Studio
From the dropdown list, choose   Show Settings
Once the settings window opens, navigate to the   Shortcuts   tab section
Find the option   Create recording flag   and modify the shortcut to your preferred key combination
