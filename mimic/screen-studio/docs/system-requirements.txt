System requirements
Minimum requirements
To ensure optimal performance when using Screen Studio, your Mac should meet the following specifications:
Operating system:
macOS Ventura 13.1 or later
Processor:
One of the following:
•  Apple M1 or later  (recommended)
•  Intel-based Mac  from these models:
▪ MacBook Pro 2018 or later
▪ MacBook Air 2020 or later
Memory:
8GB RAM minimum
Note:  Performance requirements may vary based on your recording needs. For the best experience across all recording types, we recommend using a Mac with an Apple M1 processor or later.   Checking your Mac specifications
To verify whether your Mac meets the minimum requirements:
1. Click the  Apple logo  in the top-left corner of your screen
2. Select  About This Mac  from the dropdown menu
3. Review the displayed information for:
▪ macOS version
▪ Processor type
▪ Memory (RAM) amount
The About This Mac window will show all the specifications you need to compare against the requirements above.
If your Mac meets these requirements, you're ready to install and use Screen Studio. If not, consider upgrading your hardware or macOS version for the best experience.
