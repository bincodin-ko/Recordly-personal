Zooms
Instant Zoom
Sometimes, you may want to apply an instant zoom effect without any animations to quickly focus on a specific part of your recording.
To create an instant zoom, you can either create a new zoom on your recording or select an existing zoom and click on it.
Once selected, the zoom settings window will appear on the right side of your editor.
Depending on your preferences for zoom - whether you prefer automatic or manual settings - you can choose the position where you would like the instant zoom to be applied. By toggling on the  Instant Animation.
If you wish to fine-tune the effect, you also have the option to adjust the zoom level settings.
This allows you to control how much you want to zoom in on the selected area, ensuring that the focus is exactly where you need it.
