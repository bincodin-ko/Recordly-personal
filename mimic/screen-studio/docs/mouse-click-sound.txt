Mouse click sound
To enhance your project with a polished feel, you may want to add a mouse click sound effect. Follow these steps to incorporate this effect into your video:
Open the project you want to add mouse clicks too.
In the editor, locate the   "Cursor"   tab. This can be found in the right corner of the editor.
Scroll down to find the   "Click sound"   section. Here, you will see options for mouse click sound effects.
Choose the mouse click sound effect that best fits your project. You can listen to the available options to find the one that suits your needs.
Once you have selected your desired click sound, adjust the volume level to ensure it blends well with your overall project.
After making your selections, preview the project to ensure the click sound effect enhances the viewing experience without overpowering other audio elements.
By following these steps, you can successfully add a mouse click sound effect to your project, contributing to a more engaging and polished final video.
