Recording
Managing recording in progress
During recording, you will see a small recording control widget which counts the time and allows you to control the recording.
You can use it to  finish ,  pause ,  restart , and  delete  the recording.
To hide it, right-click it and select  Hide current recording control  Please note that it won't appear in the final recording if you are using macOS 12.3 or above.
