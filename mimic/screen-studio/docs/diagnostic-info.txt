Troubleshooting
Diagnostic info
This guide explains how to share diagnostic information with our support team to help troubleshoot issues you may encounter.
Understanding Diagnostic Info
Diagnostic data includes technical information about your setup:
• Screen Studio version
• macOS version
• Mac specifications
• Connected camera details
• Microphone information
💡Note: This only includes device names and models - no content or personal data is accessed.
Sending Diagnostic Info
Open Screen Studio
In the menu bar, navigate to   Screen Studio   →   Settings
3. Switch to the   Advanced   tab
4. Scroll to the bottom of the window
5. Click the   Share diagnostics info   button
If you're logged in, your account will be automatically associated with the report. Otherwise, you'll need to provide your email address for identification.
Important Follow-up Steps
Before sending diagnostic info, ensure you:
Email our support team at  team@screen.studio
Include a detailed description of the issue you're experiencing
Without a description of the problem, diagnostic data alone may not be sufficient for our team to assist you effectively.
