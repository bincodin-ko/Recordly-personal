Presets
Sharing preset
There is an option for sharing presets.
Imagine that you have created a preset with your custom background that you want to share with your marketing team so they can apply it to new recordings made in Screen Studio.
How to share a preset
Click the   Presets   button in the editor, then open settings for the presets you want to share. Select   Open in finder   to display your preset file in Finder.
You can then copy it and send it to another user via email or messaging app.
