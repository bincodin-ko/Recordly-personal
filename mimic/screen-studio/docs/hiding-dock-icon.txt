Hiding Dock Icon
You can effectively hide the dock icon, keeping your workspace organized while still having access to Screen Studio through the top menu bar.
How to hide the dock icon
Launch the Screen Studio application.
Go to the top menu bar and click on   Screen Studio
From the dropdown menu, choose   Show Settings
In the pop-up settings window, locate the  General  tab.
When located, toggle on the   Hide Dock Icon   setting. This will ensure that the Dock icon is hidden when there are no open projects and no recordings in progress.
