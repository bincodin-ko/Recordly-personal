Adding a mask and highlight
When creating a recording, you may need to blur sensitive data or add highlights to emphasize important elements. Follow these steps to utilize masks and highlights effectively:
Start by opening your recording in the editor.
You can initiate the mask feature through the mask timeline or by pressing the shortcut key 4 to activate the mask timeline.
To apply a mask or highlight, click on the mask timeline at your desired location within the video.
Once the mask or highlight is applied, you can adjust its size or the opacity of the highlight.
💡 Masks and highlights can be applied throughout the video timeline; however, they cannot be added simultaneously within a single frame.
What are masks used for?
Masks are used to conceal specific parts of your video, effectively hiding sensitive information from viewers.
💡 Ensure that the mask covers the entire section of the timeline where sensitive data appears. The mask will not follow the screen when scrolling.
What are highlights used for?
Highlights draw attention to specific areas of your screen, helping viewers focus on important elements during your recording.
