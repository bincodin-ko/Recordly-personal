Camera Settings
You can customize your camera settings to enhance your recording experience. To apply those settings, follow these steps:
Launch the Screen Studio application on your device.
Switch on the camera in the application. Once the camera is turned on, a green camera icon will appear in the top menu bar.
Click on the green camera icon to access various webcam options. The options will include:
Background : allows you to add background wallpapers.
Portrait : focus on you in a portrait mode.
Studio Light : enhances lighting to create a professional studio effect.
