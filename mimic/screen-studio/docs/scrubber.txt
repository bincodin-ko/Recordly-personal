Editing project
Scrubber
Scrubber is an editing tool that allows you to manually navigate through a video, enabling you to listen to your voice and pinpoint exact editing points.
How to use a scrubber
Begin by opening your project in Screen Studio
Find the   scrubber   positioned above the timeline
Click on the scrubber to activate it
Move the playhead to the right to play the media forward. Drag it to the left to play it backward. This allows for precise navigation through your content.
The speed of the audio or video playback will correspond to how quickly you drag the mouse. The faster you drag, the quicker the playback.Once you've found the exact spot you need, you can perform your edits, such as deleting unwanted sections or making other adjustments.
