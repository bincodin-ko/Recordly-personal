Exporting
Shareable links
Share your videos instantly through web links - no downloads or file transfers needed. Your viewers can stream your content directly in their web browser.
This feature streamlines collaboration by: • Eliminating large file transfers • Providing instant access through any web browser • Reducing storage requirements for viewers • Enabling immediate playback
How to share my video?
Quick Share After Recording
When you finish recording, you'll see a quick share widget in the bottom-left corner of your screen with two simple options:
Click  Share  to instantly generate a streaming link
Click  Edit  to make changes before sharing
Share After Editing
If you want to polish your video first:
Select  Edit  in the share widget to open the editor
Make your desired changes
Select option to   Export   in the top menu bar and select the shareable link option
Once the export is completed, a shareable link is generated and opens immediately in your default browser, allowing for easy sharing.
This is an example shareable link:  https://screen.studio/share/ZwyKdN4t
💡Please note that currently shareable recordings have a 30 min limit. For longer projects, you’ll need to export them as a file.
How to manage your shareable links
To access all your shared videos:
Click   Screen Studio   in the top menu bar
Select   Manage Shareable Links
You will be directed to the  Shareable Links  tab on your dashboard, where you can easily access all the shareable links you have created.
