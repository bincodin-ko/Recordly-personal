Disable Automatic Zooms
Sometimes, when you make a recording, you may not want automatic zooms applied to each video and prefer to apply manual zoom for each recording instead. Follow these steps to disable the automatic zooms:
Begin by launching the Screen Studio application.
Go to the top menu bar and click on   Screen Studio.
From the dropdown menu, choose   Show Settings.
In the pop-up settings window, locate the   Recording   tab.
Find the option labeled   Create zooms automatically   and untoggle it to disable this feature.
