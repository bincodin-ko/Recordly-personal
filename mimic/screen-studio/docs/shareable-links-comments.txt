Shareable links: comments
Effectively view and leave comments on shared links, enhancing communication and feedback on your projects.
How to view comments
Start by opening the  sharable link  for which you want to view comments.
Once the link is opened, navigate to the bottom menu and select   Show Comments   or use the left side menu.
After clicking on that option, all the comments will be displayed.
How to leave a comment
In the comments section, locate the field labeled   Write a comment .  Enter your comment in this field.
Ensure that you set your  username  in the appropriate field.
If you need to change your username, look for the  pencil icon  to edit your username. Click on it, enter your desired username,
Once your username is set add your comment. To add a comment use a shortcut  ⌘ + ⏎ . This action will associate your comment with the current timestamp in the video.
