Managing License
You can easily manage your license through the dashboard. Follow these steps to view your account details and handle your licenses:
Visit the dashboard:  screen.studio/dashboard.
Log in using the email address you used for the purchase.
To reset a license, navigate to the  Device  tab within the dashboard.
Click on  Disconnect device . This action will allow you to activate the same license key on a new device.
You do not need access to your old device to reset your license key; you can perform this action from any device with internet access.
