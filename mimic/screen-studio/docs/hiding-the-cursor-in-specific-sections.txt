Hiding Cursor in Specific Sections
Sometimes, you may wish to hide the mouse cursor during certain parts of your video. This way, you can effectively manage the cursor's visibility in your video.
How to hide the cursor
Begin by opening the project you wish to edit.
Navigate to the specific cut fragment on the timeline that you want to adjust.
With the fragment selected, right-click on it to open the menu.
From the menu, select   Hide mouse cursor .
Once the change has been made, a hide arrow will appear on the selected fragment, indicating that the cursor will be hidden during that section.
