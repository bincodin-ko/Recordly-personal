Look & Feel
Camera
If you used a camera during the recording, then you can modify its position and shape visible in the final recording.
Camera settings
Hide camera   - If you prefer not to use your camera, you can hide it during recording.
Position   - You can select the position of your camera in your recording.
Mirror camera   - Use it to mirror your camera view.
Roudness   - Allows you to change the roundness of your camera frame.
Size   - Enables you to change the size of your camera frame.
Camera during zoom
There is an additional option to manage the camera size during zoom.
Usually, when there is a zoom in the recording, we decrease the size of your camera frame to avoid covering the content of your recording.
You can change the size of the camera during zooms, or you can turn it off to keep the size of your camera frame always the same.
